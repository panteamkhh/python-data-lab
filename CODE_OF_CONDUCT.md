# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want **Python Data Lab** to be a friendly, welcoming place for learners of every level. Everyone who participates — through issues, pull requests, or discussions — is expected to be respectful and constructive.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give feedback on the **code and content**, not the person.
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions.
- Keep discussions focused and on topic.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably make others feel unwelcome.

## Scope

This code applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and any communication tied to the repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block contributors who violate this code. To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately via the email on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
